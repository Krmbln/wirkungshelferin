# German-speaking (DE/AT/CH) academic and near-academic centres: impact measurement and evaluation methods

Scope: methodological, context-independent material only. Researched 2026-10-07. Our 7-step guide: 0 project context, 1 target groups, 2 problem and root causes, 3 theory of change / Wirkungslogik incl. assumptions, 4 indicators, 5 data collection methods, 6 data collection plan.

## Q1: Which centres have concrete, freely downloadable method material, and what is it?

### Takeaway
Only a few sources give a non-expert anything usable for free. The strongest are the IMV-Lab Praxishandbuch (Hamburg/LMU, 2024), the free light version of the ZEWO Impact Tool (CH), the DeGEval self-evaluation recommendations and standards glossary, and the Univation Eval-Wiki for definitions. CSI Heidelberg and WU Wien are mostly SROI-oriented, their key guides are paid books, and they don't fit a 1-hour-per-week user. CEval mainly produces academic and textbook material.

### Cited Findings

**IMV-Lab (Universität Hamburg + LMU München; Edinger-Schons et al.)**
- "Praxishandbuch Wirkung & Wirkungsmessung Sozialer Innovationen". Authors: Felizia von Schweinitz, Amyn Vogel, Ali Aslan Gümüsay, Laura Marie Edinger-Schons. Published April 2024, in German, BMBF-funded. — [EPALE entry](https://epale.ec.europa.eu/de/resource-centre/content/praxishandbuch-wirkung-wirkungsmessung-sozialer-innovationen)
- Working PDF mirror: https://epale.ec.europa.eu/system/files/2024-04/Praxishandbuch_IMV-Lab.pdf. The URL in our brief (imv-lab.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/Praxishandbuch_IMV-Lab.pdf) now returns an "IMV-Lab — Seite nicht gefunden" (404) page, checked 2026-10-07. — [EPALE](https://epale.ec.europa.eu/de/resource-centre/content/praxishandbuch-wirkung-wirkungsmessung-sozialer-innovationen)
- Contents, from the table of contents of the PDF (52 PDF pages / ~94 printed pages):
  - "Wirkungskreis" (Purpose, Vision, Mission, Strategy, Stakeholders) with a SMART figure and a stakeholder-identification approach (Abb. 5)
  - Wirkungslogik design with IOOI and Wirkungstreppe, and input/output/outcome/impact tables
  - A science-basics chapter on correlation vs causality and observation vs experiment
  - Outcome measurement via surveys and interviews: open vs closed questions, a questionnaire table, qualitative analysis, multi-item scales and index variables
  - A "learn, improve, review" step
  - Pitfalls: pseudo-precision, skipping the outcome level, monetisation, "impact netting"
  - Appendices: I checklist on the Wirkungskreis and measurement; II measurement and monetisation instruments; III and IV example outcomes and impacts with how to measure them; V levels of measurement — [PDF](https://epale.ec.europa.eu/system/files/2024-04/Praxishandbuch_IMV-Lab.pdf)
- The Anhang I checklist uses yes/no questions in blocks. Examples: "Wurde definiert, was 'Wirkung' im Kontext des Vorhabens bedeutet?", "Wurde der Umfang und die Grenzen der zu bewertenden Wirkungen festgelegt?", and "Wurden ... (SMART) Ziele definiert...?". The next block checks that purpose, mission, strategy and impact fit together. — [PDF](https://epale.ec.europa.eu/system/files/2024-04/Praxishandbuch_IMV-Lab.pdf)
- No explicit Creative Commons licence was found in the text, so it is free to download but standard copyright applies. — [PDF](https://epale.ec.europa.eu/system/files/2024-04/Praxishandbuch_IMV-Lab.pdf) (grep for licence terms found none)
- Follow-up: "Impact Readiness Guide – Wirkung verstehen, messen & steuern", November 2025, free German PDF, 22 pages: https://www.imv-lab.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Impact_Readiness_Guide_2025_DE.pdf. It is built around double materiality analysis, SDGs, CSRD/ESRS and VSME, and has three steps: identify impact fields, measure and analyse, report and steer. It links back to the Praxishandbuch. Target groups are SMEs, startups, funders and universities. — [Uni Hamburg news](https://www.uni-hamburg.de/newsroom/im-fokus/2025/1125-irg-launch.html); [PDF](https://www.imv-lab.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Impact_Readiness_Guide_2025_DE.pdf)

**ZEWO (Stiftung Zewo, Switzerland; certification body, near-academic)**
- The old "Leitfaden Wirkungsmessung" pages (zewo.ch/de/leitfaden_komplett, /wirkungsmessung_zewo) now return 404 (checked 2026-10-07). zewo.ch/de/wirkung redirects to the "Zewo Impact Tool – Wirkung zeigen" page. — [Zewo Impact Tool](https://zewo.ch/de/zewo-impact-tool-wirkung-zeigen/)
- The tool's 6 steps are:
  1. Set goals.
  2. Develop the Wirkungsmodell, "including assumptions about cause and effect".
  3. Plan measurement. Indicators must meet SMART; the tool asks questions and proposes a measurable target wording, plus an optional AI SMART-check.
  4. Collect data. A one-click "Fortschritts-Tabelle" shows which data to collect when (measurement stages, comparison type).
  5. Judge impact. Compare planned vs actual values and record steering actions.
  6. Use the learnings.
  It stresses that a baseline ("Ausgangswert") is required. — [Zewo Impact Tool](https://zewo.ch/de/zewo-impact-tool-wirkung-zeigen/)
- Pricing:
  - Light version is free for everyone: 1 user, 1 impact analysis, 1 activity, export of the Wirkungsmodell.
  - Full version is free for Zewo-certified organisations and CHF 180/year for others; it adds export of the progress table.
  - Languages: DE, FR, EN. — [Zewo Impact Tool](https://zewo.ch/de/zewo-impact-tool-wirkung-zeigen/)
- The earlier Leitfaden was aimed at development-cooperation aid organisations, described a 6-step cycle over 3–5 years, and had a resources chapter. — [search snippet of zewo.ch Leitfaden pages, now offline](https://zewo.ch/de/kapitel_01)

**DeGEval – Gesellschaft für Evaluation**
- "Empfehlungen zur Anwendung der Standards für Evaluation im Handlungsfeld der Selbstevaluation", December 2004, edited by Hildegard Müller-Kohlenberg (contributors include Beywl, Heiner, von Spiegel). Free PDF; all rights reserved. — [PDF](https://www.degeval.org/fileadmin/content/Z03_Publikationen/DeGEval_-_Empfehlungen_Selbstevaluation.pdf)
  - It defines self-evaluation as "systematische, datenbasierte Verfahren der Beschreibung und Bewertung, bei denen die praxisgestaltenden Akteure identisch sind mit den evaluierenden Akteuren". It acknowledges these are often run by people with "kaum oder wenig" training in evaluation methods. Sections: necessary framework conditions, and applying the standards to self-evaluation. — [PDF](https://www.degeval.org/fileadmin/content/Z03_Publikationen/DeGEval_-_Empfehlungen_Selbstevaluation.pdf)
- "Standards für Evaluation", first revision 2016, published Mainz 2017. The four groups are Nützlichkeit, Durchführbarkeit, Fairness and Genauigkeit. — [PDF copy (Uni Würzburg)](https://www.uni-wuerzburg.de/fileadmin/33110500/Dokumente/Evaluation/DeGEval-Standards_fuer_Evaluation_ersteRevision2016.pdf); [short version 2019](https://www.uni-wuerzburg.de/fileadmin/ext00267/Dokumente/Evaluation/DeGEval_2019_07_10_DeGEval_Standards__Kurzfassung.pdf); [DeGEval standards page](https://www.degeval.org/en/publications/degeval-standards/)
- "Glossar der Standards für Evaluation" (2017), a free 9-page PDF. It has term / definition / alternative-term columns, e.g. "Aktivitäten", "Beteiligte und Betroffene" (= Stakeholder), "Adressatinnen und Adressaten". It explicitly excludes general social-research terms. — [PDF](https://www.degeval.org/fileadmin/content/Z03_Publikationen/DeGEval-Standards/2017_11_21_DeGEval-StandardsGlossar_ES_1_.pdf)
- A related SEVAL (Swiss) paper on self-evaluation by Müller-Kohlenberg and Beywl exists. — [PDF](https://www.seval.ch/app/uploads/2017/07/w10_mueller-kohlenberg_beywl_od_selbstevaluation.pdf)
- A DeGEval Arbeitskreis Umwelt paper on an "ökologische Wirkungstreppe" (Nowack, 2025) exists. — [PDF](https://www.degeval.org/fileadmin/content/Z02_Arbeitskreise/AK_Umwelt/Publikationen/2025_Nowack_oekologische_Wirkungstreppe.pdf)

### Inferences
- The free German material clusters around the same backbone: IOOI, Wirkungstreppe, SMART, logic model. IMV-Lab and ZEWO restate what PHINEO Kursbuch Wirkung already gives us. Their extra value is narrow and specific: checklists, a causality primer, a questionnaire design, and a guided "progress table".
- The SROI-centred centres (CSI, WU) don't fit a 1-hour-per-week non-expert. SROI needs monetisation and proxies, and we already cover SROI via SVI Standards and the Matatihi guide.

### Gaps
- The DeGEval self-evaluation recommendations (pp. 9–14) could not be text-extracted (only the cover and introduction were parsed), so the exact per-standard recommendations are unverified.
- The content of the 2025 Impact Readiness Guide checklists was only skimmed (TOC and executive summary).
- The Eval-Wiki licence was not checked.
- ankerwirkmodelle.de and the evhn.de toolbox were not opened; their availability and cost are unverified.
- WZB Berlin: not researched in depth. No practical method material for nonprofits was surfaced in searches, which suggests it is not relevant.
- Universität Hamburg / TUM / Social Entrepreneurship Akademie (SRS co-authors): no further SRS-related free method material was found beyond IMV-Lab (Hamburg). TUM / SEA was not specifically checked.
- Bertelsmann's "IMMPACT" model was not found in my searches, so it is unverified.
- CSI Heidelberg's own website was not fetched directly. A free working-paper series may exist but was not confirmed.

## Q2: Which of steps 0–6 does each source inform, and what does it add beyond our catalogue?

### Takeaway
Most of the new value is in steps 0, 5 and 6. The IMV checklist works as a cross-step rubric seed. IMV's survey and interview chapter and its open vs closed questionnaire table cover step 5. The ZEWO progress table is a step 6 template that also forces a baseline. The DeGEval and Eval-Wiki glossaries are the best available seeds for our German glossary, though neither is truly plain-language.

### Cited Findings
- IMV Praxishandbuch:
  - Step 0: Wirkungskreis purpose/vision/mission/strategy and checklist block "Klares Verständnis der Wirkungen", including scope and boundaries
  - Step 1: stakeholder identification approach (Abb. 5)
  - Step 3: IOOI, Wirkungstreppe and causality chapter
  - Step 4: SMART, example outcomes and impacts with measurement suggestions (Anhang III/IV)
  - Step 5: open vs closed questions, questionnaire table, qualitative analysis, multi-item scales, levels of measurement (Anhang V)
  - Not a full data collection plan for step 6. — [PDF](https://epale.ec.europa.eu/system/files/2024-04/Praxishandbuch_IMV-Lab.pdf)
- ZEWO Impact Tool:
  - Step 3: Wirkungsmodell including cause–effect assumptions
  - Step 4: guided SMART indicator wording
  - Step 6: the "Fortschritts-Tabelle" (what data, when, which comparison; planned vs actual) and the baseline rule. — [Zewo](https://zewo.ch/de/zewo-impact-tool-wirkung-zeigen/)
- DeGEval Standards and Glossar: a quality frame across all steps (utility, feasibility, fairness, accuracy) and term definitions. The self-evaluation recommendations target exactly our user type, "practitioner = evaluator", with little training. — [Selbstevaluation PDF](https://www.degeval.org/fileadmin/content/Z03_Publikationen/DeGEval_-_Empfehlungen_Selbstevaluation.pdf); [Glossar PDF](https://www.degeval.org/fileadmin/content/Z03_Publikationen/DeGEval-Standards/2017_11_21_DeGEval-StandardsGlossar_ES_1_.pdf)
- Eval-Wiki: glossary entries with EN equivalents (e.g. Indikator), which helps map English sources (IRIS+/IMP) to German terms. — [Eval-Wiki](https://eval-wiki.org/glossar/Indikator)

### Inferences
- **Gap: rubrics/checklists for steps 0, 1, 5, 6.** IMV Anhang I is the best ready-made seed, mainly for steps 0 and 3–4. The ZEWO progress table is a template model for step 6. DeGEval's four standard groups (useful / feasible / fair / accurate) could serve as a simple 4-question quality rubric for steps 5 and 6. That is my inference, not something DeGEval prescribes for small projects.
- **Gap: intake/interview method for step 0.** None of the sources gives a structured intake interview. IMV's purpose/vision/mission/strategy questions plus its checklist block 1 can be turned into intake prompts.
- **Gap: plain-language German glossary.** The DeGEval Glossar (2017) and Eval-Wiki give authoritative German definitions but are written for evaluators. They should be used as the reference to check against, then rewritten in simpler language. The Univation A-B-C is a translation list, not a definitions glossary.
- **Already decided: evidence label for assumptions.** Nothing here conflicts with it. ZEWO and IMV both stress cause–effect assumptions but offer no labelling scheme.

### Gaps
- None of the sources offers a validated rubric scale (e.g. 1–4 levels) for any step; rubrics would have to be designed by us.

## Q3: Relevance verdict for a non-expert single German-speaking user (~1 h/week)

### Takeaway
Recommended:
- IMV Praxishandbuch: use selectively, i.e. the checklist plus the questionnaire chapter.
- ZEWO Impact Tool (Light): directly usable.
- DeGEval Glossar and Eval-Wiki: as a glossary backbone.

Background only: DeGEval self-evaluation recommendations and Standards.

Not recommended: CSI/WU SROI material, CEval papers, the IMV Impact Readiness Guide, skala-campus training, Univation A-B-C.

### Cited Findings
- **IMV Praxishandbuch: Recommended (selective).** It is free, German, practice-oriented, and contains a checklist, questionnaire guidance and outcome examples. At 94 printed pages it is too long to read whole, so point users to Anhang I and chapter 3.3. — [PDF](https://epale.ec.europa.eu/system/files/2024-04/Praxishandbuch_IMV-Lab.pdf)
- **ZEWO Impact Tool Light: Recommended.** It is a free, guided, German, step-by-step tool. Caveats: built for aid organisations, the light version is limited to 1 activity, and the progress-table export may require the paid version (CHF 180/year for non-Zewo organisations). — [Zewo](https://zewo.ch/de/zewo-impact-tool-wirkung-zeigen/)
- **DeGEval Glossar (2017) and Eval-Wiki: Recommended as reference for the glossary.** They are not for end users directly, being too technical. — [Glossar](https://www.degeval.org/fileadmin/content/Z03_Publikationen/DeGEval-Standards/2017_11_21_DeGEval-StandardsGlossar_ES_1_.pdf); [Eval-Wiki](https://eval-wiki.org/glossar/Indikator)
- **DeGEval Selbstevaluation (2004) and Standards (2016): Background only.** These are normative principles, not a step method. — [PDF](https://www.degeval.org/fileadmin/content/Z03_Publikationen/DeGEval_-_Empfehlungen_Selbstevaluation.pdf)
- **IMV Impact Readiness Guide 2025: Not recommended.** It is oriented to CSRD/ESRS/VSME and double materiality, i.e. corporate reporting. — [PDF](https://www.imv-lab.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Impact_Readiness_Guide_2025_DE.pdf)
- **CSI Heidelberg / WU Wien SROI: Not recommended.** The core guides are paid books, and SROI monetisation is beyond a 1-hour-per-week user; it is already covered by the SVI and Matatihi entries. — [Palgrave](https://palgrave.com/de/book/9783319714004)
- **CEval: Not recommended.** It produces academic working papers and textbooks rather than practitioner tools. — [SULB](https://jahrbib.sulb.uni-saarland.de/bitstream/20.500.11880/25497/1/01_Selbstdarstellung_CEval.pdf)
- **skala-campus Wirkungsmanagement: Not recommended for our use.** It is a cohort training, not downloadable method material, and its content is PHINEO, which we already have. — [Info PDF](https://www.skala-campus.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/Weiterbildung_Wirkungsmanagement-2024_Info.pdf)
- **Univation Programmbaum / A-B-C: Not recommended for end users.** The free excerpt is a bilingual term list and the Programmbaum is an evaluator tool; it is marginal next to the Wirkungstreppe and IOOI we already use. — [PDF](https://univation.org/download/Programmbaum_Landmarke_Glossar.pdf)
- **EvH Nürnberg (Ottmann): Possibly useful, unverified.** The participatory impact-model article is relevant to step 3, but it is a journal article. — [PDF](https://kidoks.bsz-bw.de/files/2590/2021_ottmann_wirkungsmodelle.pdf)

### Inferences
- If only one new German source is added to the catalogue, it should be the IMV Praxishandbuch (Anhang I checklist plus ch. 3.3 surveys/interviews). The second should be the ZEWO progress-table concept as the model for our step 6 template.

### Gaps
- The free tier of the ZEWO tool was not tested hands-on, so how usable it is for non-aid projects is unverified.
