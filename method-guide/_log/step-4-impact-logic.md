# Log step 4 – Impact logic

## Critique (draft → final)
1. **Too thin for the core step:** the draft had no example, no question sequence and no explanation of what counts as an assumption. → Fully worked out, with a 5-link example.
2. **Assumption vs. external factor blurred:** both can concern the context. A layperson won't know where to put something. → Distinction rule: assumption = what *must* hold and what we take to be true (incl. how people react); external factor = outside the project's control and may change (Goldilocks S12 p. 4–5: risks are external factors outside the organisation's control).
3. **"weil …" as an empty repetition:** "Kinder lesen mehr, weil sie mehr lesen". Since the mechanism counts as the Why (PRD F5), it must explain something. → Pitfall + rubric criterion "Mechanismus erklärt".
4. **Everything marked critical:** a layperson tends to mark everything critical, which would flood the step-5 candidate list. → Test question based on Aspen's "necessary" (S2 p. 5): "Wenn das nicht stimmt – gibt es noch einen anderen Weg zum Ziel?" Only if not → critical.
5. **Level jumps:** activity → life situation without participation/acceptance. S1 p. 36: satisfaction is necessary but not sufficient; outputs 2–3 must be in the chain. → Pitfall + rubric.
6. **Participants' perspective missing:** Vogel (S13, p. 31) recommends looking at the programme through the eyes of those meant to benefit (how do they hear about it, why take part, costs/benefits, risks). → Challenge set in part B.
7. **Contribution wording:** in line with the corrected 00-shared rule (Mayne S14), other contributing factors are recorded as external factors, not as "competing explanations".
8. **Non-focus groups:** PRD F5: they skip step 4. Their activities still belong to the project-wide activity list. → Made explicit.
