## Critique (draft → final)
1. **Missing model element:** the ToC guide (S2) knows "domino effect" (no intervention needed) and "outside our influence". Without these, the assistant would force a measure onto every link. → Added.
2. **Inputs looked like a Wirkungstreppe level:** in the draft, resources stood in the list of levels. In S1 the Wirkungstreppe starts at level 1; inputs come before it (IOOI, S1 p. 35). → Separated.
3. **Evidence basis only on assumptions:** the PRD also puts it on root causes (step 2). → Section 2 says it applies to causes and assumptions.
4. **No behaviour for "I don't know":** a layperson often has no answer. Without a rule the assistant guesses or blocks. → New rule: offer a draft, otherwise mark "offen".
5. **Shape of a proposal unclear:** PRD F4 requires a structured proposal with visible changes that can be accepted individually. → Short section added.
6. **Section 8 (summaries) is app behaviour, not method:** shortened to the behaviour towards Lukas.

- Does S1 recommend phrasing society-level goals as a contribution? **Yes.** Kursbuch p. 28: "Das Projekt trägt dazu bei, dass …".
