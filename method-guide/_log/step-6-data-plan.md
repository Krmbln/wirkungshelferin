# Log step 6 – Data collection plan

## Critique (draft → final)
1. **Too thin, no example, no instrument help:** a layperson needs concrete question templates, not just "Fragebogen". → Template questions from IMP (S10, ch. 2) and the SROI guide (S9) paraphrased in German; questionnaire rules from S1 p. 75.
2. **Effort/cost aspect in the sources:** S1 lists "Kosten vertretbar" for sources and "zeitlich und finanziell möglich" for feasibility (p. 71–72). That conflicts with the PO decision (no time/effort aspect). → Feasibility reduced to "ethically acceptable and doable for the people involved"; no cost/time estimate.
3. **Usage missing as its own question:** the draft only had "who analyses". S1 ch. 7 (analyse soon after collection) and S8 P8 (use data for decisions) call for a concrete use. → Each plan row: who analyses, on which occasion, which decision it informs.
4. **Data protection too vague:** "no consent" as a mistake without saying what's needed. → S1 p. 79: inform participants, get consent, inform parents of minors, anonymise, store safely, report results back. Plus note: not legal advice; clarify within your own organisation.
5. **Sample size:** without guidance, users start drawing samples unnecessarily. → S1 p. 77: in small projects, include everyone (full survey); for interviews, stop at saturation.
6. **Baseline timing:** step 5 plans baselines; step 6 must schedule them, otherwise they are lost. → Rubric criterion.
7. **Several indicators per row** (PRD F5): the draft talked about "per indicator". → A row can cover several indicators; every indicator needs at least one row.

## Verification
- Does S1 advise using existing data first and avoiding excessive data collection? **Yes.** Kursbuch p. 71.
- Does S1 advise including everyone in small projects? **Yes.** Kursbuch p. 77 (box on sample size).
- Does S1 require consent and informing parents of minors? **Yes.** Kursbuch p. 79 (box "Datenschutz beachten").
